Key Findings

7 of 10

Sites face HIGH cyclone or coastal flood risk

Jamnagar, Dahej, Hazira, Nagothane, Patalganga, Silvassa, and Vadodara all sit in states classified High for cyclone hazard by ThinkHazard. The Arabian Sea cyclone season (June–November) overlaps with the Indian monsoon, compounding risk for Gujarat's coastal refineries. Jamnagar's DTA and SEZ refineries together form the world's largest single-location refinery complex — a concentration of irreplaceable hard assets on a high-cyclone coastline.

All 10

Sites face HIGH extreme heat risk

ThinkHazard rates extreme heat as High for Gujarat, Maharashtra, UP, Dadra & NH, and Hoshiarpur district — covering every Reliance site in the register. Refinery and petrochemical operations are energy-intensive and rely on workers in outdoor and semi-outdoor environments. Sustained wet-bulb temperatures above 35°C can halt outdoor activity entirely. India's hottest regions are expected to warm a further 1.5–2°C by 2050 under IPCC moderate-emissions scenarios.

Scope 3 gap

Physical risk understated by Scope 3 exclusion

Reliance's physical risk register should in principle cover not just its own sites but also the infrastructure through which customers receive and use its products — pipelines, ports, petrol stations. That downstream infrastructure faces its own climate hazards. Since Reliance does not disclose Scope 3, this full chain of physical exposure is invisible in their disclosures. This risk register covers only the sites Reliance itself operates.

Gujarat water stress

Jamnagar complex most exposed to chronic water scarcity

ThinkHazard rates water scarcity as High for Gujarat. WRI Aqueduct ranks India #13 globally for baseline water stress, with Gujarat cited as one of India's most stressed states. The Jamnagar refinery complex processes approximately 1.24 million barrels of crude per day and requires large volumes of process water. Saurashtra — the peninsula where Jamnagar sits — is a semi-arid region with no perennial rivers, dependent on monsoon and groundwater.

3 inland sites

Lower coastal hazard but high heat and flood exposure

Vadodara, Naroda (Ahmedabad), and Hoshiarpur are inland, removing them from coastal flood and cyclone exposure. However, Vadodara and Naroda are in Gujarat and still rated High for extreme heat and river flood. Hoshiarpur faces medium flood and heat risk — lower overall than the coastal cluster but not negligible. Barabanki (UP) faces High river flood risk — the Ghaghra and Saryu rivers regularly breach in extreme monsoon years.

BRSR gap

No climate-related asset vulnerability assessment disclosed

Reliance's BRSR 2024-25 lists disaster preparedness as a material issue (p.13) and states that manufacturing sites are designed with a "loss prevention design philosophy." However, no quantified physical risk assessment by site is disclosed — no flood return periods, no sea-level rise scenarios, no heat threshold analysis. This register is an external independent assessment based on public hazard data; it has not been validated by Reliance.
